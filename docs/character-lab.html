Integration test scene for the male paper doll: every layer is a separate runtime sheet assembled by Phaser; the ground is a plain lab floor, not world art. Combos: unarmed J J, J J K, J K, K K K; katana J J, K K, J J K K, J K J K; wakizashi / tanto J J J (stabs), K K (swipes); dual tanto J J J (two stabs, off-hand slash), K K (main-hand, off-hand swipe). After an attack the guard (or the weapon's stance) holds for 15 s, then the idle returns; the weapon shows in its stance and attacks only (draw and sheathe are not authored yet). Hits: a 25 px arc in front of the facing (120°); damage goes to the selected target if it is inside, otherwise the closest post inside; with a target selected every attack press turns to face it.
