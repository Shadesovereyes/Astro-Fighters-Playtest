Short runs produced a cycle that wasn't real

A 60-seed grid search found one configuration that looked like a clean cycle — 58 / 58 / 52. Re-run at 250 seeds on an independent seed base, the same configuration collapsed to 51 / 49 / 49. It was noise.

At n=60 the standard error is roughly ±6.5 points, so a two-sigma swing is ±13 — larger than the entire 55–60 target band. Only the 250-seed figures on this page (±3.1 SE) are decision-grade. Treat the sensitivity sweep below as directional evidence only.

One more measurement correction

Earlier in this session I reported Wrestler vs Jiu-Jitsu as roughly even at 52/48. That run did not alternate spawn sides. With sides alternated, the real figure is Wrestler 58 / Jiu-Jitsu 42 — Jiu-Jitsu is not holding even against Wrestling, it is losing clearly. Every figure on this page alternates sides.

Why the third arrow keeps resisting

Each style's damage rests on one dominant technique, and that technique is loaded in both of its matchups. Buffing it helps one arrow and hurts the other by roughly the same amount.

  • Hip Toss is the one clean lever. It fires 228 times against Wrestler but only 41 times against Jiu-Jitsu, because Jiu-Jitsu pulls guard and drags the fight to the ground before the standing grapple matters. That makes its stamina cost a near-pure Wrestler-matchup dial — the only lever measured that moves one arrow without dragging another.
  • Sumo Palm's power number is mostly decorative. True damage is (power + STR) × 0.7, and Wrestler has STR 14. At power 5 that is (5+14)×0.7 = 13.3 per hit — the power term supplies 26% of it, the raw STR supplies 74%. That is why cutting power 5→2 earlier barely registered, and why cost and slot changes hit far harder than power changes.
  • Rock-paper-scissors normally comes from mechanics, not magnitudes. With one dominant move per style and no matchup-specific interaction, the three arrows are mathematically tied together. Arm bar being legal from the bottom is the one real asymmetry in the set — leaning on that kind of positional rule is more likely to close the loop than more number tuning.

Jiu-Jitsu is bottom of every defensive column

Lowest HP, lowest stamina pool, lowest poise, worst mitigation — and its damage type is Physical, so it eats the opponent's mitigation while Wrestling's Sumo Palm ignores mitigation entirely. Its stat block is doing none of the work its win rate needs.
