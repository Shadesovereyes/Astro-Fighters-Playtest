# stone-clean candidate v1 — Art review notes

Use beside `production/reference/world/shared-foundation/REFERENCE_SALVAGE_REVIEW.md`.

This is a **source candidate**, not an approval record.

What improved over the rejected schematic trial:

- irregular stone widths and course heights break the hidden 32×32 cadence;
- chipped corners and broken edge-lighting reduce clean-vector/flat-block appearance;
- stone values vary while staying inside one dry matte civic-stone family;
- repair/wear crosses individual stones rather than following module seams;
- moss is localized to joints rather than used as a generic fantasy/dungeon texture;
- highlight/shadow direction is consistently NW → SE.

What still requires review before promotion:

- confirm the effective pixel density is strong enough beside the established character standard;
- confirm repeated tiling does not expose a visible module boundary in Phaser;
- compare against `aged-stone-foundation` before declaring the stone palette authoritative;
- verify the full Slice 0 assembly does not read flatter than the player sprite;
- only actual Phaser capture can close integration/hidden-grid review.
